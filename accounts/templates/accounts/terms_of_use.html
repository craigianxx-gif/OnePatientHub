Terms of Use

Terms governing the use of the OnePatient Hub system.

1. Purpose of the System

OnePatient Hub is a healthcare information management prototype designed to support standardized patient identification, patient record management, healthcare facility coordination, referrals, patient journey tracking, and interoperability.

2. Authorized Use

Access to OnePatient Hub is restricted to authorized users. Users must only access information and functions that are relevant to their assigned responsibilities and permissions.

3. Patient Information

Patient information must be handled responsibly and only accessed for legitimate healthcare, administrative, technical, or authorized system-support purposes.

4. Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and must not share passwords or other authentication information with unauthorized persons.

5. System Security

Users must not attempt to bypass access controls, modify records without authorization, or interfere with the operation or security of the system.

6. Prototype Status

OnePatient Hub is currently a prototype system. Its use in a live healthcare environment requires appropriate technical validation, governance, security assessment, approvals, and integration agreements with relevant system owners.

7. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as the OnePatient Hub system evolves and its governance and operational requirements are established.
