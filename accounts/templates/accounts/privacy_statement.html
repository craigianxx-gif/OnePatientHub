Privacy Statement

Information about the handling of information within OnePatient Hub.

1. Information We Handle

OnePatient Hub may process account information, patient identification information, healthcare facility information, visit information, referral information, consent information, and system audit information as supported by the system.

2. Purpose of Processing

Information is processed to support patient identification, continuity of care, healthcare coordination, patient journey tracking, reporting, auditing, and system interoperability.

3. Access Control

Access to information is controlled through user authentication and role-based permissions. Users should only access information necessary for their authorized responsibilities.

4. Auditability

Relevant system activities may be recorded through audit logging to support accountability, monitoring, and investigation of system activity.

5. Data Protection

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be applied to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

6. Prototype Limitation

OnePatient Hub is currently a prototype. This statement does not represent a declaration that the prototype has received all regulatory, governance, security, or operational approvals required for production healthcare deployment.

7. Privacy Enquiries

Formal privacy enquiries, data-subject procedures, retention requirements, and responsible-party contacts should be established as part of the governance arrangements for any production implementation.
