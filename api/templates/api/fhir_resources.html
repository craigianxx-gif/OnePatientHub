FHIR Standardized Resources

Overview of health data models and structures supported by the OnePatient Hub national node.

👤 Patient
National patient identity management via unique OPH-ID and demographic mapping.
🏥 Organization
Healthcare facilities, hospitals, and national clinic administrative nodes.
🩺 Practitioner
Doctors, nurses, lab technicians, and certified clinical staff records.
📋 Encounter
Patient hospital visits, admissions, and consultations tracking.
🔬 Observation
Laboratory test results, diagnostic measurements, and vital signs.
📑 DiagnosticReport
Comprehensive laboratory and radiology test reports linked to patient profiles.
💊 MedicationRequest
Patient clinical prescriptions and pharmaceutical orders.
🔄 ServiceRequest
Inter-facility patient referrals and specialized service requests.
📍 Location
Specific hospital wards, departments, and regional clinic buildings.
